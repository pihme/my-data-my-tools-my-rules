Omakase: the chef's choice お任せ

The tools I actually run myself, from the catalog and beyond. One line per pick; follow the link for the full dossier.

01First courseDesktop

  • ONLYOFFICE

    opens and edits Word, Excel and PowerPoint files offline, with strong compatibility with Microsoft Office formats.

  • Syncthing

    keeps folders in sync between my devices peer to peer. A public discovery server only helps the devices find each other; the files stay on my devices.

02Second courseHome server

  • Paperless-ngx

    scans become a searchable archive with OCR and tags, kept on your own server.Still looking for something lighter: it is a bit too professional for my needs.

  • IImmich

    your own photo and video library, with search, albums and sharing, kept on your own server.

  • Navidrome

    streams your own music collection to the browser and to Subsonic-compatible apps from a single lightweight service.

  • NNixOS ↗

    the whole system is declared in one configuration file, so a setup can be rebuilt, rolled back or moved to a new machine.

  • Rrestic

    encrypted, deduplicated backups to a local disk, an SFTP server or S3-compatible storage.

  • Uptime Kuma

    checks that websites and services are reachable and sends a notification when something goes down.

  • Beszel

    lightweight dashboard for CPU, memory, disk and Docker usage across your servers, with alerts.

03DessertMobile

  • IImmich

    the Android and iOS app uploads the camera roll to my own Immich server only while I am on my home network; everywhere else I use the app offline.
