Signal provides end-to-end encrypted messaging clients. The Android client is licensed under the GNU AGPL version 3; the iOS, Desktop and server components are published in their own repositories.

## Why it fits

Clients can be installed free of charge for private communication. Admission here is client self-install, not running a private Signal network.

## Getting started

Prerequisites (from the official docs):

- A phone (Android or iOS); the desktop app is linked to a phone
- A phone number for registration

Steps:

1. Install Signal from the official download page or your app store.
2. Register with your phone number and set a PIN.
3. Optionally install Signal Desktop and link it by scanning a QR code with your phone.

Official downloads: <https://signal.org/download/>

## Notes

Self-hosting a private Signal service for personal use is not a supported offering. The license shown here is the one in the Android client repository.
