# Logos and screenshots

Tracking table for project logos and screenshots shown on the site (checked 2026-11-05).
Rule: a logo or screenshot is used only when its license or the project's brand/trademark guidelines allow showing it in a catalog to identify the project. Files are stored in `assets/projects/<slug>/`, never hotlinked, and shown unmodified (logos: no recoloring or cropping; PNG logos are only scaled proportionally). Screenshots are framed to 16:10 on a neutral background without distortion and skipped if the source is not sharp enough. When terms are unclear, the site keeps the letter tile.
