Organic Maps is a mobile maps application using OpenStreetMap data with offline map downloads. It is licensed under Apache-2.0.

## Why it fits

The app can be installed for private navigation without a paid license.

## Getting started

Prerequisites (from the official docs):

- An Android or iOS device
- Free storage for the offline maps you download

Steps:

1. Install Organic Maps from a store or download link listed on the official site.
2. Open the app and download the maps for the regions you need.
3. Search, navigate or save bookmarks; no account is required.

Official site and downloads: <https://organicmaps.app/>

## Notes

Not a self-hosted server. Privacy/no-tracking statements are project claims.
