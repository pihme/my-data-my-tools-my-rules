## Why it fits

Grayjay is a client application, not a media server. Personal/non-commercial use is permitted under Source First 1.1. The app can be installed by an individual without a paid subscription for private use (per license limitations for non-commercial purposes).

## Getting started

Prerequisites (from the official docs):

- An Android device or a Windows, macOS or Linux computer

Steps:

1. Download Grayjay for your platform from the official site.
2. Install it and enable the source plugins you want, for example YouTube or PeerTube.
3. Subscribe to creators and import existing subscriptions if offered.

Official site and downloads: <https://grayjay.app/>
