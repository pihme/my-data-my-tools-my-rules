FUTO is an organization based in Austin, Texas, founded in 2021. According to its About page, it builds and funds software "that challenges the dominance of Big Tech" through in-house engineering, investments, grants, and public education.

FUTO states that it operates without reliance on existing tech companies or venture capital, and that it holds every project it funds to three pledges: never sell out to a monopolist, never abuse customers (no "the users are our product" revenue models), and be open source or develop a plan to eventually become so.

FUTO develops some projects itself (listed on its projects page) and gives microgrants to independent projects (listed on its grants page). Some FUTO projects use FUTO's own Source First license, which is source-available but not OSI-approved, so FUTO funding alone says nothing about a project's license.
