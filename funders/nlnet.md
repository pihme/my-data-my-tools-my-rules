NLnet (Stichting NLnet) is a Dutch philanthropic non-profit foundation that has financially supported the open internet since 1997. Its roots go back to the European Unix Network (EUnet) in the 1980s. According to its foundation page, NLnet provides microgrants, advice, and access to its network, does not directly benefit from the projects it funds, and all developments are published as libre and open source.

The grants listed here come from NGI Zero (NGI0) funds such as NGI0 PET, NGI0 Discovery, and NGI0 Entrust, and from NGI Mobifree. NGI Zero is a coalition led by NLnet. As stated on each NLnet project page, these funds were established by NLnet with financial support from the European Commission's Next Generation Internet (NGI) programme. NGI Zero supports free/libre/open source software and hardware, open standards, and open data, "technologies that can be used, studied, modified and shared by anyone for any purpose".
